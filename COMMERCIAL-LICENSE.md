## Do I need to pay?

**Most users: no.**

| What you're doing | License needed |
|---|---|
| Running pkgguard locally | Free |
| Running it in your company's CI/CD pipeline | Free |
| Using it in your internal developer platform | Free |
| Forking it, modifying it, studying it | Free |
| Using it at a for-profit company, internally | **Free** |
| Offering it to customers as a hosted/managed service | **Paid** |
| Embedding it in a product you sell | **Paid** |
| Selling scanning or audit services built on it | **Paid** |

The dividing line is **redistribution and resale**, not company size or profit
motive. If you are a business protecting your own builds, use it freely. If you
are packaging it up and charging third parties for access to it, that requires
a commercial license.

(whichever comes first), that version converts automatically to
**Apache License 2.0** and all restrictions lapse.
